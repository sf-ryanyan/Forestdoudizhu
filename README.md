## 🌟 Overview · 游戏简介

Dou Dizhu is a classic Chinese card game: one **landlord** plays against two **farmers**. In Forest Dou Dizhu, you pick a cute animal from the **Forest Snack Squad** and play against two AI friends. Each match keeps going until someone runs out of pinecones — then the big winner takes the podium!

斗地主是经典的中国纸牌游戏：一位**地主**对抗两位**农民**。在森林斗地主里，你选一只「森林小吃团」的小动物入座，和两位 AI 小伙伴对战。一直打到有人松果输光，最后由大赢家登上领奖台！

## 🎮 How to Play · 怎么玩

**1️⃣ Deal · 发牌**
Each player gets 17 cards. 3 cards stay face down as the bottom cards.
每人 17 张牌，留 3 张底牌。

**2️⃣ Bid for Landlord · 叫地主**
Take turns bidding 1, 2, or 3 points, or pass. The highest bidder becomes the **landlord** and takes the 3 bottom cards. If nobody bids, the first bidder becomes landlord at 1 point.
轮流叫 1 分、2 分、3 分或不叫。叫分最高的人当**地主**，拿走 3 张底牌。都不叫时，第一个叫分的人当地主（1 分）。

**3️⃣ Double or Not · 加倍**
The two farmers choose first, then the landlord. Doubling makes your wins and losses twice as big.
两位农民先选，地主最后选。加倍后输赢都翻倍。

**4️⃣ Play Cards · 出牌**
The landlord leads. The next player must play the **same type, same number of cards, and higher rank** — or pass. If both others pass, the last player starts fresh with any type.
地主先出。下一家要出**相同牌型、相同张数、更大**的牌，或者选择「不要」。另外两家都不要时，最后出牌的人重新自由出牌。

**5️⃣ Win the Round · 赢下这局**
If the landlord plays all cards first, the landlord wins. If either farmer finishes first, both farmers win.
地主先出完牌，地主赢；任一农民先出完，两位农民一起赢。

**🔢 Card Order · 牌的大小**
`Big Joker 大王 > Small Joker 小王 > 2 > A > K > Q > J > 10 > … > 3` (suits don't matter · 花色不分大小)

## 🌰 Scoring · 计分

```
Base Score 基础分 = 20 × Bid 叫分 × 2^Bombs 炸弹数
Spring / Reverse Spring 春天 / 反春 → × 2
Doubling 加倍 → × 2 for that player · 谁加倍谁翻倍
```

- 🌸 **Spring · 春天**: the landlord wins before the farmers play a single card · 地主赢，农民一张牌都没出
- 🌸 **Reverse Spring · 反春**: the farmers win after the landlord plays only once · 农民赢，地主只出过一手
- 🏁 The match ends when any player runs out of pinecones. Most pinecones wins! · 有人松果输光就结算，松果最多的是大赢家！

## ⏱️ Time Limits · 限时规则

- The landlord gets **+10 seconds** on the first turn · 地主第一手多 **10 秒**
- Bidding **15s**, Doubling **10s** · 叫分 **15 秒**，加倍 **10 秒**
- Time's up → auto pass, or play your lowest card if you must lead · 时间到自动「不要」，轮到你自由出牌时自动出最小的一张
